1. The game
Burp Brothers is a free Android game where 3–5 players steer one table together. It has no ads, in-app purchases or microphone recording. Burps are prerecorded sounds. Bot practice works offline; friend rooms require internet access.

Linking Google preserves the guest identity when possible. 3. Rooms and friends
Share room codes with people you trust. Anyone with a code may join an open room with space. A round starts with at least three and at most five connected, ready players. Reconnection is attempted after a short interruption; the room ends if the host leaves or a connection cannot be restored. Uninterrupted service or identical performance on every device is not guaranteed. Friend connections use in-game codes and accepted requests; your address book is not accessed.
