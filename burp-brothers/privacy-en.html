BURP BROTHERS — PRIVACY POLICY
Version: online-1 | 5 November 2026
Data controller: SAO Game · info.saogame@gmail.com

Data processed
Language, sound/music and game avatar preferences, nickname and terms acceptance record (document version, language, device UTC time and explicit action) are stored on your device. Offline bot practice does not require an online account.

Google Firebase Authentication verifies a guest identity or the Google account you choose for online play. Firebase may process user identifiers, authentication tokens, technical connection data and, for Google sign-in, account profile information/email. The SAO Game game server does not store your Google password or email address. Google account selection opens only on your action. Contacts, microphone, camera and precise location access are not requested. Advertising and behavioural analytics SDKs are not used.

Authentication tokens, user ID, chosen nickname, game avatar selection, room code, readiness and gameplay inputs/state are sent to the game server. Google-linked friend profiles store the user ID, random friend code, nickname and friend request/acceptance relationships. Room participants see each other's game names and state; friends see game names and friend codes. Google email addresses are not shared with players.

Purposes and providers
Data supports authentication, friend rooms, friend requests, preferences, preventing errors/abuse and deletion requests. The game server runs on the user's Netx VDS service. Netx's official VDS page specifies Istanbul, Türkiye. Firebase Authentication uses Google's infrastructure and authentication data may be processed outside Türkiye. A game server in Türkiye does not mean all data stays in Türkiye. Google's processing information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Retention
Room and gameplay state remain in memory and are released when the room ends. Rooms last at most two hours; a disconnected player's place is reserved for at most 30 seconds. Persistent friend profiles and relationships remain until deletion is requested. The server application does not write access tokens, game audio or IP addresses to persistent access logs. Network/infrastructure providers may process their own security and legally required records. Only a deleted user ID and block expiry are retained for up to one hour to prevent an old valid token recreating a deleted profile. If backups are used, deleted data leaves the backup cycle within seven days; deletion records must be applied after restoration.

Deletion and choices
Use Settings > Account and privacy > Delete my account and data to request deletion of the game profile, friend relationships and Firebase game account. Signing in with Google again may be required for security. If deletion cannot finish across both providers, the app requests a retry; failure is not reported as completed. Your Google account itself is not deleted. You may email info.saogame@gmail.com with a Burp Brothers account deletion request without installing the app. Remove local preferences through device app-data settings; uninstalling alone does not delete the server account.

Rights and contact
Contact info.saogame@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights, including information, access, correction, deletion, learning recipients, objection to automated processing and compensation under Türkiye's Law No. 6698. Only information necessary to verify identity and process your request is requested. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents. Terms acceptance is separate from this notice and is not blanket data-processing consent.

Blocking and reports
Players, including guests, may block another player or report an in-game name/behaviour. A block record contains the two game IDs, display name and random management code; it remains until unblocking or relevant account deletion. It prevents friend requests and sharing a room with blocked players. Reports contain reporter/target game IDs, reported name, selected reason and timestamp, without free text or audio. Reports remain for up to approximately 30 days. Review may result in an account access restriction of at most 30 days. Account deletion removes associated records. These processes support safety, abuse prevention and protection of rights.
We may occasionally request the Google Play review card after sufficient use. SAO Game does not receive your rating or review; Google Play handles its own process. First use, completed round count and last request time stay on the device. The support email option opens a draft in your mail app; no email is sent until you send it.
