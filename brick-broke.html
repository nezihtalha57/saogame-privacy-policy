Updated: 25 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · Android package: com.saogame.brickbroke

Brick Broke is provided by developer Nezih Talha Karakoç under the SAO Game name. Privacy, support and data requests: info.saogame@gmail.com.

Data used by this version

The game has no account, ads, analytics, purchases, location, contacts, camera or microphone feature. Progress, stars, best scores, your local level design, an unfinished run and settings such as sound and language stay in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to a developer game server. First-use and last-review-request times are also stored locally to avoid frequent review requests.

Google Play reviews

At an appropriate break, the game may request Google's optional native review card. Availability depends on Google's installation and quota requirements. Your rating or review text is not returned to the game. Google Play handles ratings and review text under its own terms. Public reviews appear on the store; closed-test feedback may be shared privately with the developer. You can delete your review through your Google Play or Google Account. See Google Play's review data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Support email and external links

“Send feedback or a suggestion” opens an editable draft in your email app. Only if you send it do we receive your email address and message for support. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal details. Game logs are not attached automatically. Email providers deliver your message and may process it in other countries under their service terms. Privacy and store links open in your external browser or Google Play.

Purpose, retention and deletion

Local information enables gameplay and remembers your choices. Uninstalling the app or clearing its Android storage removes it, including your progress. The developer does not offer cloud backup. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to answer the request, establish/exercise/defend related rights and meet applicable legal obligations; no fixed retention period is promised. We do not create commercial ad profiles or sell data.

Your data rights

Support information you send is handled to communicate with you and address rights requests under the applicable processing condition. To request information, access, correction, deletion when applicable, or exercise other rights under Article 11 of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law, contact info.saogame@gmail.com. Only necessary identity-verification information may be requested. For Google's own processing, use Google's account tools.

Children and changes

This is an abstract brick-breaking game with no chat or player-to-player sharing. We do not ask children for personal information. If ads or other SDKs are added, the data flow, this notice and store declarations will be updated before that version is released.
