Oyun ve cihazdaki kayıtlar

Hesap, giriş, sohbet, kullanıcı içerik paylaşımı veya geliştiriciye ait oyuncu sunucusu bulunmaz. Rekor, altın, satın alınan/seçili kostüm ve arka planlar, dil/ses tercihleri, seçilen yaş grubu, ödül işlemi kaydı ve değerlendirme istemi zamanları uygulamanın cihazdaki özel alanında saklanır. Tam doğum tarihi istenmez. Bu oyun kayıtları geliştiriciye yüklenmez; eğme kontrolü için kullanılan ivmeölçer verisi oyun içinde anlık işlenir ve gönderilmez.

Yerel kayıt ve yedeği cihazdan uygulama verisi temizlenerek veya uygulama kaldırılarak silinebilir; bunun altın ve kozmetik ilerlemesini de sileceğini unutmayın. Bulut kayıt/cihazlar arası aktarım hizmeti sunulmaz. Yerel tercihler ve ilerleme, oyunun istenen işlevlerini sunmak için işlenir.

Çocuklar, gençler ve yaşını belirtmeyen oyuncular

Tarafsız yaş ekranında 13 yaşından küçük, 13–17, 18 ve üzeri veya belirtmeden devam seçilebilir. Oyunun kullanılabilirliği bu seçime bağlı değildir. 18 yaşından küçük ve yaşı bilinmeyen oyuncular için reklam SDK'sı oyun tarafından başlatılmaz ve reklam isteği yapılmaz. İkinci şans ve 5 kat altın seçenekleri bu oyunculara reklamsız sunulur. Bu sürüm çocuklardan reklam kimliği talep etmez.

Yetişkinlerde isteğe bağlı reklamlar

18 yaş ve üzeri seçilmişse Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 ve User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0 kullanılır. Reklamlar yalnız oyuncu ilgili ödül düğmesini seçtiğinde gösterilir. Otomatik açılış reklamı ve oyun içi banner yoktur. Kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam isteği ve G içerik filtresi kullanılır. Android reklam kimliği ile AdServices reklam/konu/atıf izinleri manifestten çıkarılmıştır; diğer SDK verileri bundan dolayı ortadan kalkmaz.

English

This policy describes Bouncy Cevahir for Android, provided by Nezih Talha Karakoç under the SAO Game brand. Developer and privacy/data-controller contact: info.saogame@gmail.com.

Game and local progress

The game has no accounts, login, chat, user content sharing or developer-operated player server. Scores, coins, owned/selected cosmetics, language/sound preferences, the selected age group, reward transaction marker and review prompt timestamps stay in the app's private device storage. The game does not ask for a full birth date or upload these save files. Accelerometer readings used for tilt controls are processed locally and are not transmitted.

You can remove local progress and its backup by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. This also deletes coins and cosmetics. There is no cloud save or cross-device migration service. Local preferences and progress enable the requested game functions.

Children, teens and unknown ages

A neutral screen offers under 13, ages 13–17, 18 or older, or continuing without choosing. Every choice can play the game. For players under 18 and players of unknown age, the game does not initialize the advertising SDK or request ads. These players receive the same second-chance and five-times-coins options without an ad. This release does not request an advertising identifier from children.

Optional adult advertising

For users who choose 18 or older, the game uses Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 and UMP 4.0.0. Ads are shown only after selecting an optional reward button. There are no automatic launch ads or in-game banners. Requests use non-personalized ads and a G content filter. Android advertising ID and AdServices advertising/topics/attribution permissions are removed. Other SDK data still exists.

Google's SDK can collect/share IP addresses and inferred approximate location, product/ad interactions such as views and taps, diagnostic/performance data, and device/other identifiers including app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The app does not request precise location. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Read Google's SDK disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

Privacy choices and processing outside Türkiye

UMP updates consent status on eligible adult launches and displays any required regional privacy message. Ads are requested only when the SDK allows them. When required, ad privacy choices can be reopened from Help and privacy. Declining to watch an ad does not prevent normal gameplay or remove earned coins. Connection/consent/ad availability failures do not lock the game.

Google and its service providers may process data outside Türkiye. Advertising and consent processing must follow applicable regional permissions and provider data protection arrangements. UMP alone does not establish compliance with all Turkish KVKK obligations or international-transfer conditions. Contact the privacy address for details of the applicable processing/transfer arrangements.

Reviews and feedback

Google Play In-App Review 2.0.2 may request Google's review flow at an appropriate moment. The developer does not receive your rating or review text from this SDK and does not reward reviews. Store and feedback links are available to everyone. Feedback opens an email draft that you edit and send yourself; the app does not automatically send mail or logs. Voluntarily sent contact/message details are used to handle your support request.

Retention, deletion and rights

Local data remains until you clear it. There is no developer player account/database or account deletion service. Google's SDK retention/deletion practices follow its policies; the developer cannot promise a fixed retention period or direct deletion control over those systems. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; contact the privacy address for access/deletion requests.

Under applicable Turkish data protection law, you may request information about processing, purposes and recipients, correction, deletion/destruction when statutory conditions apply, notification of relevant changes to recipients, objection to adverse solely automated analysis, and compensation for unlawful processing. Contact info.saogame@gmail.com. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information, passwords or full device logs.
