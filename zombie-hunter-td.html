Publisher and scope

SAO Game publishes Zombie Hunter TD for Android, package com.saogame.zombiehuntertd. This policy covers the Android app. Contact us for support or privacy questions at info.saogame@gmail.com.

Gameplay and local storage

You can play without an account or an internet connection. Gold, permanent upgrades, highest unlocked level, a random local save identifier, FPS preferences and the advertising age group you select are stored on your device. This Android version does not provide account creation, Google sign-in, cloud saves, an online leaderboard, chat or in-app purchases. We do not upload your game save to a developer server.

Advertisements

The app uses Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertisements require an internet connection. An interstitial may appear at a level break when you choose to continue; it is subject to a frequency limit. Rewarded advertisements are optional. Completing one adds three times the level's normal gold reward, making the total four times the normal reward, once per completed level. You can continue without viewing a rewarded advertisement. Unavailable advertisements do not block gameplay.

Google's SDKs may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and application information, permitted device identifiers, advertisement interactions and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and reliability. The data processed depends on your device, applicable restrictions and privacy choices. Restricted or non-personalized advertisements can still involve network data processing. We do not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location access, and do not add a separate developer analytics service.

Age groups and privacy choices

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and contains fantasy combat against zombies. Before initializing advertisements, we ask whether you are under 16, 16 or older, or prefer not to say. This selection stays on the device; we do not ask for your name, birth date or identity documents. Under-16 and unspecified selections receive Google's more restricted child/under-age-of-consent advertising treatment. For the 16-or-older selection we apply teen advertising treatment. Advertisements have a maximum requested Google content rating of PG.

Where required, UMP presents the relevant consent or privacy message. When required by the SDK, Settings provides an advertising privacy preferences control to review or withdraw choices. You can use the game without advertisements when no eligible ad is available. The age selection does not change gameplay rewards or access. If local law considers a player a child, applicable child privacy protections remain relevant regardless of a store age label. A parent or guardian may contact us with concerns; avoid sending unnecessary information about a child.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear application storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Android backup is disabled for this app. Clearing storage also removes your local preferences and progress; we cannot remotely recover or erase data held only on your device. Google controls the retention of information it processes under its own policies.

If you email support, we process your email address and the information you provide to respond. To request deletion of support correspondence or other information you sent us, email info.saogame@gmail.com with the subject “Zombie Hunter TD data deletion.” Specify the information to delete without including unnecessary sensitive information. We may retain information needed to comply with legal obligations or resolve a dispute. This Android version has no app account to delete.

Third-party services and changes

See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information for Google's practices. GitHub Pages hosts this page and may process access information under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This page contains no advertising or tracking scripts.

We will update this policy and its effective date if data practices change. New features such as accounts or cloud saves require corresponding updates before release.
