Destek ve başvuru yazışmaları talebin karşılanması, ilgili sorunun çözümü ve uygulanabilir hukuki/kayıt saklama gereklilikleri için gerekli süreyle sınırlı tutulur. İşleme sebebi ortadan kalktığında ve zorunlu saklama gereği bulunmadığında silme veya anonimleştirme talepleri değerlendirilir. Google ve GitHub'ın kendi kayıtları için sağlayıcıların saklama ve silme süreçleri geçerlidir. Uygulama depolamasını temizlemek sağlayıcı kayıtlarını otomatik kaldırmaz.

  • Yalnızca otomatik analiz sonucunda aleyhinize bir sonucun ortaya çıkmasına itiraz etme,
  • Kanuna aykırı işleme nedeniyle zarara uğramışsanız giderilmesini talep etme

This notice provides information under Article 10 of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 for Monster Attack: Defend Your Home, package com.saogame.monsterattack. Data, collection methods and purposes

Game progress, resources, weapons, inventory, rewarded-ad progress, language/audio/FPS preferences and advertising age-group selection are saved locally on the device. They are not uploaded to a SAO Game server. Where these records constitute personal data, records needed to provide the game service rely on Article 5(2)(c), performance of the service.

Google's advertising SDK processes network/device/app identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, interactions and diagnostics through electronic ad requests. Purposes are non-personalized ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Device access requiring consent under applicable law relies on consent under Article 5(1); this version does not request personalized advertising. Security and fraud-prevention processing may rely on Article 5(2)(f) only where necessary and without overriding fundamental rights and freedoms. Each basis is limited to its relevant purpose and necessary data; non-personalized advertising does not automatically remove consent requirements.

Support email provides the sender address, optional name, message, attachments and any version/language/chapter information left in the editable draft. Handling the requested support relies on Article 5(2)(f), legitimate interests subject to the rights balance, or Article 5(2)(c) where directly related to performance of the service. Privacy-rights correspondence and proportionate identity verification are used for legal obligations under Article 5(2)(ç), and where necessary establishing, exercising or protecting rights under Article 5(2)(e). GitHub collects policy-site visitor IP addresses for hosting security under its own service notice.

The app has no accounts, cloud saves, online rankings, chat or purchases. It does not request GPS, contacts, camera or microphone permissions or special-category data. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

Google and its relevant advertising service partners receive ad/network/device data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Google's Gmail handles support and rights correspondence. SAO Game does not operate a service for selling local game saves or support correspondence.

These providers may process data outside Türkiye. International transfers are subject to the conditions of Article 9 for the relevant transfer: an adequacy decision, an appropriate safeguard, or an occasional-transfer exception only where its conditions actually apply. This notice is not a standard contract, transfer safeguard or consent document. Using a provider alone does not establish those conditions. Provider notices are linked in the privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until app storage is cleared or the app is removed; operating-system backups may keep copies. Clearing storage removes progress and rewards, and SAO Game cannot remotely erase device-only records. Support and rights correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and relevant legal or record-keeping requirements. Deletion or anonymization requests are evaluated when the processing purpose ends and no mandatory retention remains. Google and GitHub have their own retention and deletion processes; clearing app storage does not automatically delete their records.
